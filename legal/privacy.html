Privacy Policy

Hazard Studio · Effective date: August 27, 2026

This is the general privacy policy for apps and websites published by Hazard Studio, the independent app studio of Vitali Mikhailouski. Where an app has its own privacy policy (linked from its App Store page and from its page on this site), that policy applies to the app; this document covers everything else and states the principles all our apps follow.

Our principles

  • Local first. Personal content you create in our apps (entries, check-ins, preferences) is stored on your device, not on our servers, unless an app's own policy says otherwise.
  • No selling data. We never sell your personal data.
  • Minimal collection. We collect only what a feature needs, and ask for system permissions (location, health, notifications, tracking) at the moment they are needed.

This website

hazard.studio is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not set cookies and do not run analytics on it. GitHub may log standard technical request data (such as IP addresses) to operate the hosting service — see GitHub's privacy statement. Pages use fonts served by Google Fonts; font requests are made to Google's servers.

Our apps

Our iOS apps may use third-party services for analytics, attribution, and subscription management (such as Amplitude, AppsFlyer, Firebase, and Adapty), tied to a random identifier rather than your identity. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. Each app's own policy lists the specifics.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Deleting an app removes its locally stored data; for anything else, contact us and we will help.

Children

Our apps and this site are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always available at this page. Material changes will be reflected by the effective date above.
