Privacy Policy

Solara — Daily Energy Forecast · Effective date: September 2, 2026

This policy describes how Solara ("the app", "we") handles your information. Solara is a Hazard Studio app, operated by Vitali Mikhailouski (individual developer). We build Solara to work with as little personal data as possible.

Data processed on your device

Your birth date, interests, daily check-ins, and preference settings are stored locally on your device and are used to personalize your daily reading (biorhythms, numerology, insights). They are not uploaded to our servers.

Location

With your permission, the app uses your device location to request weather, air-quality and geomagnetic data for your area. Coordinates are sent to weather data providers (Open-Meteo, NOAA) solely to retrieve forecasts and are not stored by us. You can deny location access and the app will use a default city.

Health data

If you grant access to Apple Health, the app reads only the categories you approve — sleep, steps, heart rate, heart rate variability and, on iOS 18 and later, State of Mind entries — and attaches them to your daily log on your device. The app does not write to Apple Health. Health data is processed on your device, is never uploaded to our servers, and is never used for advertising, analytics, or shared with third parties. You can revoke access anytime in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive your payment details. Purchase status (which plan is active, trial and renewal dates) is processed by our subscription-management provider, Adapty, to unlock features, prevent fraud and measure subscription performance. It is tied to a random identifier, not to your name.

Analytics

To understand how the app is used and improve it, we collect anonymized usage events (screens viewed, features used, onboarding steps) and basic device information (model, OS version, language) through Mixpanel. Quiz answers are sent only as categories (for example "sleep" as a goal); free-text answers are never sent. This data is tied to a random identifier generated on your device, not to your name, email or contacts.

No tracking

Solara does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you install the app from one of our ads, the campaign that brought the install may be recorded at the campaign level; this is not linked to your identity.

Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications (morning forecast, evening check-in). You can disable them anytime in Settings.

Your choices

  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.
  • You can withdraw location, health, and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • To request deletion of analytics data associated with your random identifier, contact us.

Children

Solara is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Not medical advice

Solara provides wellness and lifestyle content for informational and entertainment purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always available at this page. Material changes will be reflected by the effective date above.
